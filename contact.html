Common Questions

How do we start working together?

Send me a message through the contact form with your project details. I'll get back within 24 hours to schedule a discovery call where we discuss requirements, timeline, and budget.

What is your typical project timeline?

Timelines vary by scope. A simple landing page takes 1-2 weeks, a fullstack MVP takes 4-6 weeks, and complex enterprise apps can take 2-3 months. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our discovery call.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes! I offer monthly retainer packages for maintenance, updates, bug fixes, and feature additions. This ensures your application stays secure and up-to-date.

What technologies do you specialize in?

My core stack is React/Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, and AWS. I'm also experienced with Python, GraphQL, Docker, and various other modern tools. I choose the right tech for each project.
